# Projectile Motion

Projectile motion is the movement of an object launched into the air that moves under the influence of gravity (assuming air resistance is negligible).


E.g:

A foot kicked into the air
A basketball thrown toward a hoop

Horizontal motion (X-axis): Constant velocity.

Vertical motion (Y-axis): Accelerated motion due to gravity.

Horizontal velocity: \(V_x\)



Vertical velocity: \(V_y\)
